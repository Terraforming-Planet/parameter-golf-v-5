# V5 Compliance Notes

## Official challenge alignment

- Official challenge-facing work in this repository is centered on the fixed FineWeb evaluation path.
- Record-track claims must remain reproducible under the challenge limits (including runtime and artifact size).

## Separation of official and auxiliary work

- Custom or auxiliary datasets are maintained as a separate experimental track.
- Auxiliary datasets are not treated as official challenge benchmarks unless explicitly validated for compatibility.
- Any future compatibility claim must be documented with clear methodology and evidence.
